# Paper trail

Paper trail is the parent folder for **inactive records retained for future
reference**. It is not a task list, reply queue, or place to hide unreviewed mail.

Retain a message when its record, evidence, personal significance, or likely
future use justifies keeping it. If it still requires a reply, action, active
tracking, or upcoming-event access, keep it in the appropriate workflow folder
until that purpose ends.

## Categories

| Subfolder | Keep here |
| --- | --- |
| Personal | Personally significant correspondence and records without a better fit below |
| Financial | Bills, invoices, banking, tax records, and other financial administration |
| Agreements | Evidence of terms, promises, approvals, or commitments made by you or someone else |
| Purchases | Order history, purchase receipts, warranties, returns, and product-related records |
| Travel | Trip-related correspondence, itineraries, bookings, and travel records |
| Official | Government, public-authority, and administrative correspondence |

Agreements includes the former Commitments category. It preserves proof; it does
not track whether a live obligation has been fulfilled. Active obligations
remain in Action context or Discussions as appropriate.

## Choosing one destination

Ask: **What would I look for this message to prove or retrieve later?**
Choose the category that best answers that question. Sender identity alone does
not determine the destination.

Use these examples as tie-breakers, not additional automated filtering rules:

| Record | Destination and reason |
| --- | --- |
| Hotel reservation for an upcoming trip | Events while needed for upcoming logistics; Travel when retained afterward |
| Hotel invoice kept as a trip record | Travel |
| Hotel invoice kept chiefly for tax or reimbursement evidence | Financial |
| Product receipt kept for a return or warranty | Purchases |
| Utility invoice | Financial |
| Government payment notice kept to document an administrative case | Official |
| Government payment notice kept chiefly as a tax or payment record | Financial |
| Written promise or accepted contract terms | Agreements |
| Personally meaningful message with no other retention purpose | Personal |

Overlap is normal. Choose the primary retrieval purpose rather than creating more
folders or deliberately filing duplicates. Search can help when a record could
plausibly fit another category.

## Monthly review and exit rule

Review Paper trail and its subfolders monthly to keep retention intentional:

- Keep records that still justify retention.
- Move misfiled records to the better category.
- Move records no longer needed to Trash.

Age alone is not a reason to delete a record. There is no universal retention
period in this workflow and no automatic purge. Applicable recordkeeping needs
remain part of the manual retention decision.

Extracting an attachment or copying useful text elsewhere does not necessarily
replace the original email's evidence, context, or provenance.
